# Design system

Notion-inspired editorial workspace. Off-white navigation rail, white reading surface, charcoal type, thin neutral dividers. Three featured project spotlights pair editorial context with actual short excerpts on sage, sand, and rose panels. Native disclosure sections reveal reader focus, structure, and previews. A compact, initially collapsed library retains all twelve samples and category filters. An explicitly labelled independent content plan demonstrates a proposed reader journey, distribution, and measurement approach.

Typography: DM Sans for navigation, headings and body; Libre Caslon Display for editorial emphasis. Desktop uses a fixed 238px sidebar and a centered reading column. At 760px, navigation becomes a dismissible menu and the work collection becomes one column. Visible keyboard focus and reduced-motion support are included.

Portfolio filters use verified article topics; all twelve sample links open original Google Docs. Featured writing can be assessed through short excerpts and contextual previews before leaving the site. Confirmed services and email are included. Lead positioning is Health & Wellness Content Writer. No strategy outcomes or client credentials are claimed. A persistent theme toggle switches between light and charcoal dark modes, initially following the visitor's system preference. Editorial panels retain accessible sage and warm brown equivalents in dark mode.
