# Design system

Notion-inspired editorial workspace. Off-white navigation rail, white reading surface, charcoal type, thin neutral dividers. The work leads, with two editorial covers and three quieter document entries. Muted sage, sand and rose identify article categories.

Typography: DM Sans for navigation, headings and body; Libre Caslon Display for editorial emphasis. Desktop uses a fixed 238px sidebar and a centered reading column. At 760px, navigation becomes a dismissible menu and the work collection becomes one column. Visible keyboard focus and reduced-motion support are included.

Portfolio filters use verified article topics; sample links open original Google Docs. Contact details remain an explicit placeholder until supplied. The content strategy positioning follows the user's confirmed target role; no strategy outcomes or client credentials are claimed.
